💡 How to Conduct a Self-Inspection for Your Private Car

Many insurance companies now allow self-inspection videos for car insurance renewals or claims. Follow these steps:

  1. Ensure your car is parked in a well-lit area.
  2. Use your smartphone to record a 360-degree video of your car.
  3. Capture close-up shots of the chassis number, engine, and existing damages (if any).
  4. Submit the video via your insurer’s mobile app or website.
